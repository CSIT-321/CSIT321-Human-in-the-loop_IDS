Five traps

  1. Search obeys the active band. Click ALL 5,000 first or you get “No alerts match”.
  2. The queue subtitle is stale. It says “band, then operational score”. The order is severity, then score. Do not read it aloud.
  3. “Record verdict” moves down ~90px once you pick a verdict. Scroll, then click. If nothing happened, nothing was recorded.
  4. “This alert is Critical” prints on Medium/High/Low alerts. Known defect — see the answer at right.
  5. Act 8 step text claims −0.020. The console shows ±0.000. Do not read that step.

If the panel asks

“Was AL-00478 really Critical? The badge says HIGH.”
Two measures, only the wording conflates them. The floor triggers on the score: is_critical means the detection score reached 80, and this alert scored 99.89. The badge is capped by the attack class — since v1.29 a Web Attack cannot display above High however high it scores. Measured on data/demo.db, is_critical matches combined_score >= 80 on every alert with zero mismatches; the highest unscored alert is 42.77. The trigger is right — the sentence is stale. Do not call the trigger “too wide.”

“Why did 975 alerts not move when you confirmed them?”
They already sit at the maximum 100 and scores are bounded. +10 applies as +0.04. It is why the evaluation’s two guardrail arms came out identical — no policy bound on that sequence; the 100 ceiling did, on all 40 verdicts.

“Corroborated reads 0 but 200 alerts have rule + model?”
A band and an evidence class are different labels. All 200 sit in the Tier 2 band, so the Corroborated band is empty. The tab reads RULE + MODEL 200. Both are right.
