What this is

Detection is an offline batch over recorded CIC flow data. Every flow becomes an alert — including the ones no detector flagged, which form the queue's bottom band and the evaluation's denominator. An analyst works the queue, records a verdict, and the verdict does not merely re-score that one alert: it updates the alert's family, and the family's learned adjustment is read by alerts that arrive later. The safety claim is the guardrails — a bounded score change, floors that protect critical findings, and an append-only trail of who decided what.
