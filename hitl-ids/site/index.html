What this is

The operational system correlates Suricata EVE records, assigns a system attack classification and priority, and routes to Tier 1 review or a formal Tier 2 Case. Analysts share an exclusive work queue. Canonical Verdicts and Investigation Conclusions feed automatic Exact Memory; safe policy controls reuse in Selective Review. Response decisions remain human-authorized and are never executed by this product. All decisions retain an append-only audit trail.
