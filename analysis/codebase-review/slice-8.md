## Challenges to the team's assumptions

- **"The V2 console replaced the legacy analyst pages" is true for analysts, not for administrators.** Four legacy surfaces remain mounted: `/admin/status`, `/admin/guardrails`, `/admin/audit` and `/admin/reports/ip`. The first three are labelled historical or are linked from Evaluation & Audit; the IP report is not. A final product must either remove them or list them explicitly as retained research surfaces; leaving an unlabelled legacy report inside `/admin` contradicts the "no dead generations" goal (S8-01).
- **The evaluator role is described two ways.** `auth.py:45-46` says the evaluator seed remains "only for historical/academic compatibility", and `UsersPage.tsx:20` hides evaluator accounts from "current product users"; but `roles.ts:55-68` gives the evaluator primary nav entries for five current operational pages (Tier 2 Cases, Triage Memory, Selective Review, Incoming Alerts, Alert Triage), and `App.tsx:112-117` mounts them. Decide whether the evaluator is a current read-only observer or a research account; the nav and the Users page currently disagree.
- **Role enforcement is not uniform on the read-only research surfaces.** Every listed admin *current* endpoint is server-gated (admin) and every evaluator Final V3.1 endpoint is admin/evaluator, so the React guards are not the only guard there. But the historical audit source `/api/audit-log` accepts any authenticated role (routes.py:584-588; the API's own test reads CONFIG_CHANGE entries as the analyst, tests/test_api.py:587-596), and `/api/evaluation/runs*` and `/api/evaluation/scenarios` are any-auth (routes.py:643-645, 680-682, 704-706) while the evaluator UI presents Metrics/Scenarios/Run as evaluator-only. If those restrictions matter, move them to the API; if the reads are intentionally shared, stop presenting the pages as role-exclusive. The legacy feedback write goes the other way (S8-04): it is open to any role while the UI it once served is gone.
- **The Overview page's availability tiles are constants, not measurements.** `AdminOverview` sets `workspace_ready=True, database_ready=True` unconditionally (admin_current_routes.py:34-35), and OverviewPage renders them as "Database availability" and "Workspace availability" (OverviewPage.tsx:28-30). The subtitle's "not production health probes" caveat is honest, but the tiles cannot report a degraded workspace; a final product needs a real readiness probe or should drop the tiles.
- **`StatusPage`'s own docstring says "nothing here computes a number of its own" (StatusPage.tsx:11-15), but the "Unresolved" tile sums `new + claimed + in_progress` (lines 74-77), the one computed figure the test even names.** This is a small example of the documentation drift that will matter when the architecture/UI documents are rewritten (see S8-10 and S8-02).
