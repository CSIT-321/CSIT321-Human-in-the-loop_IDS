contexts, 40 learning examples and 160 new Holdout identities. It is not production SOC traffic,
a human-participant study or a detector/classifier accuracy benchmark. Three synthetic Analyst
accounts model authenticated feedback provenance, not measured human time or cognition. Generalization
beyond these patterns, long-term expiry, productivity, staffing and statistical significance were not
measured. A first contradiction can be deferred until authoritative human evidence arrives.

(37/140)** in the pre-registered primary population of this controlled 200-alert evaluation, producing
**37 safe deferrals and 0 primary unsafe deferrals**. Across all 160 Holdout alerts there were **2 unsafe
