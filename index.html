Detection that learns from the analyst.

A hybrid signature + XGBoost intrusion detection pipeline fused into one explainable 0–100 risk score — then refined by structured analyst feedback under safety guardrails.

Analyst verdicts are captured once and reused: repeated false positives get suppressed, true positives rank higher, and guardrails guarantee critical alerts can never be silenced. Every decision lands in an append-only audit log.

01 · Problem

Alert fatigue is a human problem, not a detection problem

IDS tools raise alerts far faster than analysts can review them — and a large share are false positives. Every unnecessary re-review taxes attention, and the noise trains people to stop trusting the tool exactly when it matters.

/01

High Alert Volume

Queues grow faster than review capacity; priority ordering becomes guesswork.

/02

False Positives

The same benign activity is flagged again and again, session after session.

/03

Alert Fatigue

Repeated low-value alerts erode focus, trust, and response quality.

/04

No Learning Loop

Analyst verdicts evaporate — the dashboard never adapts from them.

02 · Solution

Six verdicts. One adjusted score. Zero silenced threats.

Two complementary detectors score every network flow. The analyst's verdict then adjusts the fused risk score within guardrailed limits — and the decision is reused across similar alerts.

SCORE ADJUSTMENT · 86 → 41 · FLOOR ENFORCED

  • Hybrid detection — signature rules catch known patterns; an XGBoost model scores anomalous flows.
  • Alert fusion — both detectors combine into one 0–100 explainable risk score.
  • Structured feedback — six verdict categories drive deterministic score adjustment.
  • Safety guardrails — score floors, ceilings, and critical-alert protection stop feedback from hiding real threats.
  • Similar-alert grouping — one decision suppresses an entire family of repeated false positives.
  • Append-only audit log — every decision and score change stays traceable, forever.

03 · Features

Built around review, feedback, and evidence

F·01

Hybrid Detection Engine

Signature rules and a supervised ML model classify flows in parallel, providing complementary evidence for every alert.

F·02

Fused Risk Scoring

Detector outputs combine into a single 0–100 score that assigns and adjusts alert priority across the queue.

F·03

Explainable Alerts

Signature evidence and SHAP feature attributions on every alert — the analyst sees exactly why it fired.

F·04

Human Feedback Loop

Six structured verdicts with feedback history, amendment, and bulk actions for high-volume triage.

F·05

Safety Guardrails

Admin-configurable floors, ceilings, and critical-alert protection ensure feedback never hides a real threat.

F·06

Audit & Reporting

Append-only audit logging plus exportable reports of alert activity, trends, and review performance.

07 · Scope

Clear boundaries for the FYP timeline

In Scope

  • Hybrid signature + ML detection on recorded flows
  • Fused 0–100 risk scoring with explanations
  • Structured analyst feedback & score adjustment
  • Safety guardrails & append-only audit log
  • Offline evaluation vs. no-feedback baseline

Out of Scope

  • Live packet capture / real-time inspection
  • Automated blocking or response
  • Host-based / endpoint detection
  • SIEM / SOAR integration
  • Online model retraining

Success Criteria

  • Fewer repeated false positives than baseline
  • Improved ranking of true positives
  • Zero suppression of protected critical alerts
  • Explainable, traceable score changes
  • Usable review workflow for analysts
